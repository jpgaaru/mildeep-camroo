Governance & UI Laws

UI Laws & WCAG Accessibility Principles

Established psychological laws and international accessibility standards governing the Camaroo design system.

  • 1. Fitts's Law (Target Acquisition Time). All interactive controls (buttons, selects, switches) maintain a minimum touch target of 48px height with pill borders, making navigation effortless and reducing miss-clicks.
  • 2. Hick's Law (Decision Time). Exactly one primary teal action is permitted per viewport. Complex dropdowns utilize search filters and progressive categorization to prevent choice paralysis.
  • 3. WCAG 2.1 Contrast Standards (AA & AAA). Body text adheres to a minimum 4.5:1 contrast against surfaces; deep shades (--teal-800, --teal-900) provide 7:1 AAA contrast on white canvas.
  • 4. Law of Proximity (Gestalt Psychology). Form labels, inputs, and validation feedback are grouped into cohesive .field-block units with 6px micro-gaps, separating them from unrelated adjacent fields.
  • 5. Jakob's Law (User Expectation). The collapsible sidebar uses familiar left-docked island geometry, rotating chevrons, and instant hover flyouts matching world-class enterprise SaaS standards.
  • 6. State Completeness Law. Every component ships with Default, Hover, Active, Focus, Disabled, Loading (Skeleton/Spinner), and Empty states.
